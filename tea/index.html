Green Teas

Gyokuro Fujinami Brothy, umami-rich flavor, with subtle sweetness
Asatsuyu Sencha Sweet, refreshing and grassy finish
Hojicha Roasty, sweet, nutty

Pu Erh

Jasmine Pearl Sheng Elegant, floral, with a musky finish
Black Sticky Rice Bold, murky, sticky rice (haha)

Infusions

Barley Bold, nutty, similar body to coffee
Corn Sweet, refreshing

Signature Syrups

Classic Flavors

Strawberry Versatile, can be used in mixed drinks or tea
Mango All around yummy, great on deserts, in drinks, or on its own
Lemon Bright, sour, also versatile
Cara Cara Orange Sweet, less citrus than lemon

Seasonal Specialties

Apple (usually red) Warm with a kick of fall
Kumquat (with zest) Bright, citrus zing, little sour
Sugar Bee Melon Subtle, somehow savory?, lightly sweet
Guava Amazing, tropical, sweet
