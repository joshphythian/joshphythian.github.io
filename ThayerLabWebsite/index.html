Our Research

Our lab focuses on p53: the tumor supressor protein. When it mutates, it ceases to function, implicated in over 50% of human cancers. So, we develop new methods and software to understand it better. including Sector Analysis, Markov State and electrostatic models.

Goals

We want to uncover the fundamental mechanisms that govern protein regulation, allosteric signaling, and misfunction in disease. Specifically, we study how proteins operate at the atomic level and how molecular failures contribute to pathological states.

Our Bread and Butter

Molecular Dynamics Simulations, Machine Learning, Structural Bioinformatics, Mathematical Modeling, Sector Analysis
