WATERBOY

dir. Michael Fadugbagbe

SUPPORT

My name is Michael Fadugbagbe, a Nigerian-American artist and filmmaker from Jamaica, Queens and writer-director of WATERBOY.

This film began 6 years ago in the state of Massachusetts. It was my first year at Deerfield Academy, a New England boarding school, and my parents had forbade me from playing with the football team.

I became their waterboy.

Deerfield was my first time outside of a strict & religious family. We never listened to worldly music, just Gospel, Bluegrass, and African praise.

Away from Queens, filling water and collecting jerseys, I inadvertently developed a love of music.

Cold Deerfield winters brought about interest in the strategy of sport, the power of movement, and the distance between order and chaos.

Last fall we successfully crowdfunded over $1,500 for production, it was given generously and spent prudently. We're asking for help again.

Costs like finishing and festival fees are producing a financial mountain.

We have to raise $2500 to conquer the mountain.

Festival fees limit access to first-generation and working class filmmakers like myself, and those I graduated with.

I know we are in turbulent times.

I know we are in trying times.

I believe art creates our future.

Thank you for taking the time to read, donate, and share.

Peace

Script Synopsis:

One day before playing the best team in their Conference, the worst high school football team in the state of Connecticut must choose between the brutish strategy of their coach or the chaotic one of their waterboy.
