about

Hello! I'm Lauren Tran-Muchowski (she/they), a writing and audio enthusiast with a passion for journalism, podcast production, and editorial writing. I graduated with Honors from Wesleyan University with B.A. in American Studies and English with a certificate in Social, Cultural, and Critical Theory.

While at Wes, I was the Public Affairs Director of WESU, co-coordinator of the Wesleyan Doula Project, and co-host of Engage Radio, a collaboration with the Jewett Center for Community Partnerships and WESU. My work has appeared on WHYY, NPR's The World, Voice of America, and KALW. A deep appreciator of language, I am passionate about informing the public, deconstructing social norms, and showcasing underrepresented voices.

If there is a project you'd like to see come to fruition, please send me an email, and let's make it happen!

And if there's something you think I should be listening or reading, feel free to send an email as well. ♡
