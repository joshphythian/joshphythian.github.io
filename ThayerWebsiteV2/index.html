Research

Focus

p53: the Tumor Supressor Protein.

Mutated p53 is implicated in over 50% of human cancers

We develop new methods and software to understand it better

Goals

Uncover the fundamental mechanisms that govern protein regulation, allosteric signaling, and misfunction in disease.

Our Approach

Molecular Dynamics Simulations, Machine Learning, Structural Bioinformatics, Mathematical Modeling, Sector Analysis
